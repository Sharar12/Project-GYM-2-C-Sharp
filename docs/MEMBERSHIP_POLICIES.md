# Gym Membership Policies & Billing Reference

## 📋 Membership Tiers
1. **Basic**: Access to gym floor during off-peak hours (10:00 - 16:00).
2. **Standard**: Full gym access, locker room, and steam room.
3. **Premium**: Full gym access + weekly personal training session + group classes.
4. **VIP**: Unlimited 24/7 access + dedicated trainer + monthly body composition scan.

## 💳 Payment & Invoicing
- Monthly billing cycles start from registration date.
- Grace period: 7 calendar days before access card deactivation.
