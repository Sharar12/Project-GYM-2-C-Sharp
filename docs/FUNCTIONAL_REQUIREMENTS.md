# Functional Requirements Specification (FRS)

## 1. Member Management Portal
- Self-registration and front-desk onboarding.
- Status tracking: Active, Expired, Frozen, Cancelled.
- Profile details: Emergency contacts, dietary requirements, assigned fitness coach.

## 2. Staff & Employee Operations
- Role-based permissions for Coaches, Front Desk Staff, and System Administrators.
- Shift management and personal trainer client rosters.

## 3. Financial & Facilities Module
- Subscription tier billing (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum).
- Locker rentals and biometric attendance tracking.
